      About this game

      Chimpanzee That! is an unofficial fan-made trivia game about the radio shows, podcasts and audiobooks of Ricky Gervais, Stephen Merchant and Karl Pilkington.

      Every question, Rockbusters clue and quote was written from the episode transcripts and then checked a second time against the exact lines it came from. After you answer, the game shows you those lines. The questions cover XFM (2001 to 2005), the 2005 BBC Radio 2 specials, the Guardian podcasts and Podfathers (2005 to 2006), the Fame and NME specials, and the Ricky Gervais Guides (2008 to 2011). Each answer tells you which episode it came from, so you can go and listen to the bit.

      Scores and privacy

      There are no accounts and nothing is sent to a server. Your name, points and badges are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. A challenge link carries the round, the question seed, your name and your score, so a friend can play the same questions.
